- Mesh + clips: Mixamo "Vanguard" / Soldier via three.js examples
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb

  https://github.com/readyplayerme/animation-library
  Licensed for use with Ready Player Me avatars. Baked into this file.
